⚠️ Urgent Clinical Symptoms Guidance

Do you have any of the following acute symptoms?

If any item below applies, do not wait for your period to finish or for a numerical PBAC threshold. Seek immediate medical evaluation.

Clinical Advisory:

Acute hemodynamic compromise requires urgent hospital or clinic attention. Numerical PBAC screening is intended for stable menstrual monitoring, not acute emergency triaging.

⚙️ PBAC Scoring Configuration

Simplified PBAC Scoring Rules:

  • Lightly stained pad: 1 point
  • Moderately stained pad: 5 points
  • Fully soaked pad: 20 points
  • Small blood clot (≤ 1 cm): 1 point
  • Large blood clot (> 1 cm): 5 points
  • Flooding episode: 5 points

Data Management:

Remove all recorded PBAC bleeding assessments, pad counts, and cycle history. Resets the application to a clean initial state.
